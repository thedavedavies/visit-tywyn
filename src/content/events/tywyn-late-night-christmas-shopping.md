Tywyn's independent High Street stays open late on Thursday 26 November, from 5pm to 7pm, for an evening of Christmas shopping. Nearly 30 shops, cafes and local businesses are taking part, many with festive food and drink on, gift vouchers to buy, or Christmas orders to take. With the town's lights already on, it is a good evening to tick off a few presents and support the local independents at the same time.

## Who is taking part

### Food and drink

- [Toast](/eating/toast-coffee-shop/): Christmas food specials, boozy hot chocolate and mulled wine, coffee and food gift boxes and gift vouchers, with Dovey Driftwood gifts on sale and live music from Leigh and Holly.
- Harbour Kitchen Pizzeria: a feature cocktail and mulled cider, a festive pizza and Black Forest gateau waffles, plus gift vouchers.
- Layla's Lounge: Christmas drinks and food specials, and gifts to buy.
- Cegin Fach: food specials including jacket potatoes, chilli con carne and hot dogs, plus gift vouchers. Mel Nain Lleol is there too, selling local honey and beeswax candles.
- [Mor Tywyn](/eating/mor-tywyn/): a festive Christmas box alongside the usual menu.
- Chippys Fish Bar: a festive pie and chips alongside the usual menu.
- [Proper Gander](/eating/proper-gander/): open for evening meals.

### Christmas food to pre-order

- John Rowlands Butchers: Christmas and New Year meat.
- Pantri Dysynni: Christmas and New Year meat and vegetables, plus food to pick up on the night.
- Morawel LL36: flowers, plants, gifts and food, with Christmas wreaths to order.

### Gifts, books and clothes

- Wood & Wool: gifts and cards, and a glass of Buck's Fizz for every customer.
- Love to Create: gifts and cards, with sweets and chocolates for customers.
- Clock Tower Books: new and secondhand books.
- Kelsey's Sweets: cards, gifts and sweets.
- The Captain's Locker: antiques and vintage.
- Tywyn Trends: clothing.
- OAN Nail Studio: handmade candles from Storylight Candles.
- Totaleigh Music: gift vouchers.
- Red Cross: Christmas gifts and more.
- Air Ambulance: Christmas gifts, with mulled wine and mince pies.

### Beauty and pampering

- Serenity Beauty: Christmas gifts and vouchers, and a free five-minute hand massage.
- Milestones Photography: mini photo sessions at Serenity Beauty, booked in advance only.
- The Beauty Room: gift vouchers.
- Sizzers: Danielle is at Layla's Lounge with hair products and brushes.

### Laundry and dry cleaning

- HCH Laundry: Christmas gift vouchers.
- Scrubadub Laundry Tywyn: open, with money off dry cleaning drop-offs.

Offers are as the businesses announced them, so check with a shop if you are making a special trip for something.
